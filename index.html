02 // GROUP IDENTITY

MEET CORTIS

THE NAME ORIGIN
"COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES"

The name CORTIS is derived from six letters taken directly from the phrase "Color Outside the Lines". Formed under BIGHIT MUSIC in 2025, the group symbolizes a commitment to free creative expression, unconstrained by societal standards or rigid industry templates.

THE FIVE-MEMBER CREATIVE ENGINE

Composed of MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON, and KEONHO, CORTIS represents a self-driven generation of artists who write, produce, and choreograph their own material.

04 // CREATIVE JOURNEY

THE STORY OF GO!

01

THE 2-YEAR ALBUM R&D PROCESS

BIGHIT MUSIC developed the album COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES through a rigorous two-year creative journey. During this period, more than 300 candidate demo tracks were written and evaluated by the members and production team before selecting the final five tracks.

02

CORTIS MEMBER PARTICIPATION

Unlike traditional group formations, all five members of CORTIS actively took part in writing lyrics, crafting melodies, and creating the high-energy choreography for "GO!".

03

SONG MEANING & INTERPRETATION

"GO!" stands as an explosive rally cry. It captures the decisive moment when doubts are discarded and momentum takes over—encouraging listeners to leap forward without hesitation.
